About

I hold a PhD in Finance from the University of Zurich, including a visiting stay at Chicago Booth, and an MSc in Statistics from ETH Zurich. My training is in time series econometrics, statistics, and machine learning, with published research on inflation forecasting, factor-model inference, and news-based macro signals.

Since 2020, I have been working at the Swiss National Bank as a Quantitative Macro Researcher, where I build forecasting models for inflation and real activity. Alongside standard macroeconomic data, I work extensively with news and alternative data, using NLP and LLMs to extract signals from it and running the resulting models live in production. This work has fed into executive briefings and presentations, as well as monetary policy decisions.
