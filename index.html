About

I spent the last six years at the Swiss National Bank turning unconventional data into macroeconomic forecasts. My work spans the full chain: scraping news and alternative data, extracting signals with NLP and LLMs, running the models live in production, and standing behind the resulting views in front of the people who set policy.

On the research side, I hold a PhD in Finance from the University of Zurich, with a visiting stay at Chicago Booth, and an MSc in Statistics from ETH Zurich. My published research covers machine learning methods for inflation forecasting, factor-model inference, and news-based macro signals.
